# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Course authors working primarily on desktop who need to assemble structured lessons, reusable content, exercises, and local media without depending on a hosted service.

## Product Purpose

Asakiri Studio is a focused, local-first course editor. It gives authors a dependable project workspace where content and media remain portable, inspectable, and under their control.

## Brand Personality

Focused, capable, and calm. The interface should feel like a trustworthy creative tool that stays out of the author's way.

## Anti-references

Avoid cloud-dashboard styling, decorative AI-tool aesthetics, murky tinted dark themes, ornamental glow, drop shadows, glassmorphism, and interfaces that imply publishing, synchronization, or persistent Git workflows.

## Design Principles

- Keep the author in the course-making workflow.
- Make local ownership and project scope legible without making file management the product.
- Prefer familiar, durable editing conventions over novelty.
- Use visual emphasis only when it communicates hierarchy, state, or action.
- Keep content and media reusable without obscuring where references come from.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Preserve full keyboard access, visible focus, sufficient text and control contrast, semantic structure, reduced-motion behavior, and localization-safe layouts.
